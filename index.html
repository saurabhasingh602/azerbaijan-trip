Family trip · late winter

Azerbaijan, from the Caspian to the Caucasus

Seven unhurried days through Baku, the Absheron fire sites, Shamakhi, Gabala and silk-road Sheki, paced around naps, feeds and warm van rides.

Dates
8 – 14 Mar 2027
Travellers
5 adults · 1 infant
Bases
Baku 4 nights · Gabala 2
Weather
Baku 2–10 °C · snow up high

The route

Two bases, six nights. Keep the same Baku hotel for both stays and leave the big bags there while you head west.

  1. Days 1–4 · 3 nightsBakuOld City, Gobustan, fire sites
  2. ~3.5 h drive via ShamakhiGabalaDays 4–6 · 2 nights · Sheki trip
  3. ~3.5 h drive backBakuDays 6–7 · 1 night · fly home

Day by day

Drive times are approximate and assume a private van with driver. Each day links to its route in Google Maps.

01Mon 8 MarBaku

Day 1: Arrive in Baku

  • Arrival: land at Heydar Aliyev Airport (GYD); private van with a pre-booked infant car seat.
  • Afternoon: check in and rest. Nothing else planned.
  • Evening: a short stroll on the Caspian Boulevard as the Flame Towers light up.

With baby: Baku is GMT+4. Keep day one empty so the baby can settle.

02Tue 9 MarBaku

Day 2: Old City & Highland Park

  • Morning: Icherisheher, the walled Old City (UNESCO): Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs.
  • Lunch: around Fountain Square, then a wander down Nizami Street.
  • Late afternoon: funicular up to Highland Park for sunset over the bay.

With baby: The Old City is cobbled and hilly. A baby carrier works better than the stroller.

03Wed 10 MarBaku · Absheron

Day 3: Rock art & the land of fire

  • Morning: Gobustan rock-art museum and petroglyphs (UNESCO); optional mud volcanoes nearby.
  • Afternoon: Bibi-Heybat Mosque on the way back, then Ateshgah, the old fire temple at Surakhani.
  • Dusk: Yanar Dag, the hillside that has burned for decades.

With baby: The mud-volcano track is very bumpy; baby and one adult can wait at the Gobustan museum.

04Thu 11 MarBaku → Gabala

Day 4: Shamakhi on the way west

  • Morning: head west; stop at the Diri Baba Mausoleum, built into a cliff at Maraza.
  • Lunch: Shamakhi's Juma Mosque, and an optional winery visit.
  • Afternoon: on to Gabala and check in among the forested Caucasus foothills.

With baby: Split the drive into three legs that fit around feeds.

05Fri 12 MarGabala · Sheki trip

Day 5: Silk-road Sheki

  • Morning: the Palace of the Sheki Khans and its stained-glass shebeke windows.
  • Lunch: piti stew and Sheki halva near the old caravanserai.
  • Afternoon: optional stop at the Kish Albanian church, then back to Gabala.

With baby: The palace interior is small. Go in two groups if it is busy.

06Sat 13 MarGabala → Baku

Day 6: Snow views, then farewell

  • Morning: Tufandag resort gondola for snowy views, or a calm walk at Nohur Lake if the weather turns.
  • Afternoon: drive back to Baku.
  • Evening: farewell dinner with live mugham music.

With baby: Ride the enclosed gondola cabins, not the open chairlifts.

Day 7: Last look and departure

  • Morning: the Heydar Aliyev Center, or Taza Bazaar for dried fruit, saffron and tea, if your flight is later in the day.

    Trip essentials

    Pick your window

    Option A

    Mid to late February

    Deepest snow at Tufandag, quiet roads and sights, lower hotel rates. Baku is at its coldest and windiest.

    Option B · suggested

    Early to mid March

    Milder in Baku, still snow up high, and the run-up to Novruz, with bonfires on the Tuesday evenings before the holiday.

    Heads-up

    Novruz holidays, ~20 March

    A multi-day public holiday. Festive, but hotels fill early and some offices and shops close.

    Before you go

    E-visa for all six

    Apply on the official ASAN Visa portal, evisa.gov.az. The infant needs their own application. Standard processing is about three working days; apply two to three weeks out and print every visa.

    Check eligibility and the fee for your nationality on the portal.

    Passports

    Every traveller, the baby included, needs a passport valid well beyond the return date; six months of validity is the safe rule. Carry the baby's birth certificate too.

    Flying with the baby

    Book the baby as a lap infant and request a bassinet row. Most airlines check a stroller and car seat free.

    Travel insurance

    Cover all six, with medical cover for the infant and winter-sports cover for anyone skiing.

    Weather & packing

    Baku · Caspian coast2–10 °CWindy, occasional rain
    Gabala & Sheki · foothills−2–9 °CFrosty mornings, snow on the peaks
    Tufandag · upper slopesBelow 0 °CSnow and wind chill

    For the adults

    • Windproof, waterproof jacket and thermal layers
    • Insulated, grippy boots for snow and wet cobbles
    • Hat, gloves and a scarf
    • A modest layer or scarf for mosques
    • Universal adapter: type C/F plugs, 220 V

    For the baby

    • Snowsuit, warm layers, mittens and an ear-covering hat
    • Soft carrier plus a compact stroller
    • Own infant car seat, unless the van company confirms one in writing
    • Formula or familiar foods for the first days
    • Baby medicines, thermometer and prescriptions

    Stays & getting around

    Baku · 4 nights

    One home base

    A three-bedroom serviced apartment or a hotel near the Boulevard and Old City, kept for both Baku stays.

    Gabala · 2 nights

    Resort with a pool

    A family resort near Tufandag. Ideas to compare: Qafqaz Riverside, Qafqaz Tufandag Mountain Resort.

    Room set-up

    Two doubles and one triple, or one apartment. Ask in writing for a baby cot at every property.

    Private van with driver

    A 7–8 seat van fits six people and luggage. Confirm winter tyres and an infant seat. In Baku, Bolt taxis are easy but rarely have child seats.

    Good to know

    Money
    Azerbaijani manat (AZN). Cards work widely in Baku; carry cash for the regions and bazaars.
    Time
    GMT+4, no daylight saving.
    Language
    Azerbaijani; Russian is widely understood; English in hotels and central Baku.
    Mobile data
    Local SIMs (Azercell, Bakcell) at the airport, or an eSIM bought before you fly.
    Water
    Use bottled water, especially for formula.
    Emergencies
    112 general emergency · 103 ambulance · 102 police · 101 fire.
