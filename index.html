Trip essentials

Azerbaijan, from the Caspian to the snow

Ten unhurried days through Baku, the Shahdag mountains, Gabala and Sheki, planned around naps, feeds and warm van rides.

Suggested dates 8 Mar – 17 Mar 2027
Travellers 5 adults · 1 infant
Weather Baku 2–10 °C · snow up high
Pace One big outing a day

The route

Three bases, nine nights. Baku is home: leave the big bags at the same Baku hotel and travel light into the mountains.

  1. Days 1–4 Baku 3 nights
  2. ~3 h drive
  3. Days 4–6 Shahdag 2 nights · snow
  4. ~3 h drive
  5. Days 6–7 Baku 1 night
  6. ~3.5 h drive
  7. Days 7–9 Gabala 2 nights · Sheki trip
  8. ~3.5 h drive
  9. Days 9–10 Baku 1 night · fly home

Day by day

Drive times are approximate and assume a private van with driver.

01 Mon 8 Mar
Baku

Arrive in Baku

  • Arrival — land at Heydar Aliyev Airport (GYD); private van with a pre-booked infant car seat.
  • Afternoon — check in and rest. Nothing else planned.
  • Evening — short stroll on the Caspian Boulevard to watch the Flame Towers light up.

With baby: Baku is GMT+4. Keep day one empty so the baby can settle.

~30 min transfer

02 Tue 9 Mar
Baku

Old City & Highland Park

  • Morning — Icherisheher, the walled Old City (UNESCO): Maiden Tower and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs.
  • Lunch — around Fountain Square, then a wander down Nizami Street.
  • Late afternoon — funicular up to Highland Park for the view over the bay at sunset.

With baby: The Old City is cobbled and hilly. A baby carrier works better than the stroller.

On foot + taxi

03 Wed 10 Mar
Baku

Gobustan & modern Baku

  • Morning — Gobustan rock-art museum and petroglyphs (UNESCO), south of the city.
  • Optional — the mud volcanoes nearby, reached in local off-road cars.
  • Afternoon — Bibi-Heybat Mosque on the way back, then the Heydar Aliyev Center.

With baby: The mud volcano track is very bumpy. The baby and one adult can wait at the Gobustan museum.

~1 h each way

04 Thu 11 Mar
Baku → Shahdag

Up into the mountains

  • Morning — leave Baku after breakfast; photo stop at the striped Candy Cane Mountains near Khizi.
  • Lunch — roadside stop in Quba district.
  • Afternoon — check in at Shahdag Mountain Resort; first snow play and sledging near the hotel.

With baby: It is well below zero up here. Put the snowsuit on before the last leg of the drive.

~3 h + stops

05 Fri 12 Mar
Shahdag

Snow day & the Red Village

  • Morning — ski lessons or lift rides for the adults who want them; snow walks for everyone else.
  • Afternoon — down to Quba town and Qırmızı Qəsəbə, the historic Red Village of the Mountain Jews.
  • Evening — fireside dinner back at the resort.

With baby: Take turns so one adult is always indoors and warm with the baby.

~45 min to Quba

06 Sat 13 Mar
Shahdag → Baku

Land of fire

  • Morning — slow breakfast, then drive back to Baku with a lunch stop.
  • Late afternoon — Ateshgah, the old fire temple at Surakhani.
  • Evening — Yanar Dag, the hillside that has been burning for decades, best after dark.

With baby: Yanar Dag is open and windy. Keep it to a short visit and warm up in the van.

~3 h + 1 h loop

07 Sun 14 Mar
Baku → Gabala

Shamakhi on the way west

  • Morning — head west; stop at the Diri Baba Mausoleum, built into a cliff at Maraza.
  • Lunch — Shamakhi: the Juma Mosque, and an optional winery visit and tasting.
  • Afternoon — continue to Gabala and check in among the forested Caucasus foothills.

With baby: Split the drive into three legs that fit around feeds.

~3.5 h + stops

08 Mon 15 Mar
Gabala · Sheki trip

Silk-road Sheki

  • Morning — drive to Sheki for the Palace of the Sheki Khans (UNESCO) and its stained-glass shebeke windows.
  • Lunch — piti stew and Sheki halva near the old caravanserai.
  • Afternoon — optional detour to the Kish Albanian church, then back to Gabala.

With baby: The palace interior is small. Go in two groups if it is busy.

~1.5 h each way

09 Tue 16 Mar
Gabala → Baku

Cable car, then farewell

  • Morning — Tufandag resort cable car for snowy views, or a calm walk at Nohur Lake if the weather turns.
  • Afternoon — drive back to Baku.
  • Evening — farewell dinner with live mugham music.

With baby: Ride the enclosed gondola cabins, not the open chairlifts.

~3.5 h drive

10 Wed 17 Mar
Baku → home

Bazaar and departure

  • Morning — if the flight allows, Taza Bazaar for dried fruit, saffron, tea and pomegranate sauce.
  • Departure — transfer to GYD and arrive three hours before the flight.

With baby: Ask again for the bassinet seat at check-in and gate-check the stroller.

~30 min transfer

Azerbaijan · 5 adults + 1 infant

Trip essentials

Paperwork, packing, rooms and rides, sorted before the first flight is booked.

Pick your window

Option A

Mid to late February

Deepest snow at Shahdag and Tufandag, quiet roads and sights, lower hotel rates. Baku is at its coldest and windiest.

Option B · default

Early to mid March

Milder in Baku, still snow up high, and the run-up to Novruz: bonfires on the Tuesday evenings before the spring holiday.

Heads-up

Novruz holidays, ~20 March

A multi-day public holiday. Festive, but hotels fill early and some offices and shops close. Book well ahead if your dates overlap.

Before you go

E-visa for all six

Apply online through the official ASAN Visa portal, evisa.gov.az. The infant needs their own application. Standard processing is about three working days; apply two to three weeks out and print every visa.

Check eligibility and the fee for your nationality on the portal.

Passports

Every traveller, the baby included, needs a passport valid well beyond the return date; six months of validity is the safe rule. Carry the baby's birth certificate too.

Flying with the baby

Book the baby as a lap infant and request a bassinet row when booking. Seat the group together around it. Most airlines check a stroller and car seat free.

Travel insurance

Cover all six, with medical cover for the infant and winter-sports cover for anyone skiing at Shahdag.

Weather & packing

Typical daytime ranges, approximate. Baku's wind makes it feel colder than the number.

Baku · Caspian coast 2–10 °C Windy, occasional rain
Gabala & Sheki · foothills −2–9 °C Frosty mornings, snow on the peaks
Shahdag · mountains Below 0 °C Snow on the ground, wind chill on the slopes

For the adults

  • Windproof, waterproof jacket and thermal base layers
  • Insulated, grippy boots for snow and wet cobbles
  • Hat, gloves and a scarf or neck gaiter
  • A modest layer or scarf for mosques
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen for the snow
  • Universal adapter: type C/F plugs, 220 V

For the baby

  • Snowsuit, warm layers, mittens and a hat that covers the ears
  • Soft carrier (for the Old City and Sheki) plus a compact stroller
  • Own infant car seat, unless the van company confirms one in writing
  • Formula or familiar foods for the first few days
  • Baby medicines, thermometer and any prescriptions
  • A few days of nappies and wipes; more are sold in city supermarkets

Stays & getting around

Baku · 5 nights

One home base

A three-bedroom serviced apartment or a hotel near the Boulevard and Old City. Keep the same place for all three Baku stays and store the big bags there.

Shahdag · 2 nights

Ski-in resort hotel

Stay inside Shahdag Mountain Resort so snow, food and warmth are all a short walk apart. Ideas to compare: Pik Palace, Shahdag Hotel & Spa.

Gabala · 2 nights

Resort with a pool

A family resort near Tufandag keeps day 9's cable car close. Ideas to compare: Qafqaz Riverside, Qafqaz Tufandag Mountain Resort.

Room set-up

Three rooms — two doubles and one triple — or one apartment. Ask for a baby cot in writing at every property, plus a kettle or bottle-warmer if needed.

Private van with driver

A 7–8 seat van for the airport runs and every intercity day fits six people and luggage. Confirm winter tyres for the mountains and an infant seat. In Baku, Bolt taxis are easy but rarely have child seats.

Good to know

Money
Azerbaijani manat (AZN). Cards work widely in Baku; carry cash for the regions and bazaars.
Time
GMT+4, no daylight saving.
Language
Azerbaijani; Russian is widely understood; English in hotels and central Baku.
Mobile data
Local SIMs (Azercell, Bakcell) at the airport, or an eSIM bought before you fly.
Water
Use bottled water, especially for formula.
Pharmacies
Look for "Aptek"; plentiful in Baku, sparse in the mountains, so stock up before day 4.
